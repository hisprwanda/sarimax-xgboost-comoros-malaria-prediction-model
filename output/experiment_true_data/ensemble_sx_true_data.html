Findings
Local data vs GEE proxy: This experiment replaces satellite-derived GEE climate data with ground-truth station readings. Differences vs the original 7-district benchmark reflect both the change in climate data source and the reduction to 3 districts with full local coverage.
SARIMAX leads the ensemble on this dataset (CRPS 24.176 vs ensemble 24.847). With 78 training weeks (~18 months), XGBoost has limited data to learn non-linear feature interactions across only 3 districts. The autoregressive structure of the local data is strong enough that SARIMAX alone is competitive. Retraining with 2+ years of data per district is expected to restore the ensemble advantage.
Short series caveat: The original benchmark pooled 7 districts × 78 weeks. Here we have 3 districts × 78 weeks. XGBoost sees 3× fewer training rows, making its non-linear patterns harder to learn reliably. PI coverage is still well-calibrated at 77.7% (target 80%) confirming the probabilistic outputs are trustworthy.
