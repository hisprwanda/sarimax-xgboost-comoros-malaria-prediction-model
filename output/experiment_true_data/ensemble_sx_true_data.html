Findings
Local data vs GEE proxy: This experiment replaces the Google Earth Engine satellite-derived climate data with ground-truth station readings for the same districts. The quality of climate signal in the training data directly affects feature correlation and model accuracy. Differences in CRPS vs the original 7-district benchmark reflect both the change in climate data source and the reduction to 3 districts.
Ensemble behaviour: The Ensemble S+X benefit is visible when one component underperforms on a specific district — the other compensates. Check the per-district CRPS table above: districts where XGBoost CRPS < SARIMAX CRPS indicate non-linear climate signals; districts where SARIMAX CRPS < XGBoost CRPS indicate strong autoregressive patterns dominating.
Short series caveat: With 78 training weeks (~18 months), the XGBoost component has limited data to learn non-linear feature interactions. The original benchmark used 7 districts × 78 weeks pooled. Future retraining with 2+ years of data per district is recommended to stabilise XGBoost feature importance.
