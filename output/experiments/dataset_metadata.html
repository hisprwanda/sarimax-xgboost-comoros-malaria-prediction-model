Comoros Malaria — Climate-Health Training Dataset

Weekly malaria surveillance records from 7 districts of the Ngadjizi region, Union of the Comoros. Collected January 2024 – December 2025 (2 full years). Used to train and evaluate the Ensemble S+X probabilistic forecasting model across a nine-experiment benchmark series.

Data Provenance & Context
Where the data comes from and what it represents
Disease data: Weekly aggregated malaria case counts sourced from the district-level Health Management Information System (HMIS) of the Union of the Comoros, covering the Ngadjizi region (Grande Comore island). Cases reflect confirmed and clinically diagnosed malaria (primarily Plasmodium falciparum), reported by health facilities within each district catchment area.
Climate data: Weekly mean rainfall (mm/week), mean temperature (°C), and relative humidity (%) derived from ERA5 reanalysis data (ECMWF) aggregated to district level. Climate records are provided as contemporaneous observations (not forecasts) for the training and evaluation periods.
Research context: This dataset was assembled as part of the Climate-Health Analytics Platform (CHAP) pilot for the Union of the Comoros. The 9-experiment benchmark series was designed to identify the best probabilistic forecasting model for operational malaria early-warning at district level. Column Schema — 6 fields per record
All columns present in evaluation_data.csv
time_period
STRING
ISO 8601 week identifier in YYYY-Www format (e.g. 2024-W01). Spans 2024-W01 to 2025-W52 — 104 consecutive weeks with no gaps.
Range: 2024-W01 → 2025-W52
location
STRING
District identifier — one of 7 named districts in the Ngadjizi region, Grande Comore. Each district is modelled independently; no spatial pooling is applied.
Cardinality: 7 unique values
disease_cases
INTEGER
Weekly malaria case count for the district. This is the forecast target. Non-negative integer; 8 zero-case weeks observed (Mitsamiouli only).
Global range: 0 – 1,393 cases/week
rainfall
FLOAT
Mean weekly rainfall (mm/week), ERA5 reanalysis aggregated to district centroid. Highly seasonal — near-zero in dry season, up to 68.5 mm/week in wet season.
Range: 0.00 – 68.53 mm/week
mean_temperature
FLOAT
Weekly mean 2-metre air temperature (°C), ERA5. Comoros has a tropical oceanic climate — temperature varies by ~4–6 °C across the year. Coastal districts (Hamahamet, Mitsamiouli) are warmer than inland highlands.
Range: 19.66 – 27.50 °C
humidity
FLOAT
Weekly mean relative humidity (%), ERA5. Consistently high across all districts (68–90%), reflecting the humid tropical climate. Peaks during the monsoon wet season (Jan–Mar).
Range: 67.93 – 90.49 %

Train / Test Split
Fixed temporal split — no shuffling, no cross-contamination
🟦 Training set
2024-W01 → 2025-W26
Weeks 1 – 78  ·  78 weeks  ·  546 records  ·  ~75% of data
🟧 Test set
2025-W27 → 2025-W52
Weeks 79 – 104  ·  26 weeks  ·  182 records  ·  ~25% of data
The split is purely temporal — the model is trained on the first 78 weeks and evaluated on the last 26 (a full half-year). This mimics operational deployment: you always predict into the future. Feature-selection cross-correlations and all data-driven choices are computed exclusively on the training split to prevent leakage.
