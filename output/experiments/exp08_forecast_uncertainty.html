Exp 08 — Forecast Covariate Uncertainty

How much does malaria forecast skill degrade when climate drivers carry forecast error?

Comoros · 7 districts · models fitted once · 30 Monte Carlo noise realisations per scenario · n_samples=50

Key Finding

Models are fitted once on historical data; only the future climate inputs are varied. Under short-range forecast noise (±15% rainfall, ±0.8°C, ±4% humidity), CRPS degradation is minimal for all models (<3%). Under seasonal-range noise (±50% rainfall, ±2.5°C), degradation reaches ~10-18%. The Ensemble S+X champion degrades least in proportional terms: its SARIMAX component relies on lagged climate signals (which are partially exact from the training tail), while its XGBoost component captures non-linear signal even under noise. These results confirm the model is operationally viable for 1-8 week health early-warning.
